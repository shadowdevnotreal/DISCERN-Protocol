1
👁️ Recognition
Seeing the Truth Clearly

🎯 Goals

  • Acknowledge patterns of harm, toxicity, or dysfunction
  • Understand that change is necessary for your wellbeing
  • Begin exploring liberation as a viable path
  • Reach out for information and support

🔑 Key Activities

• Self-assessment and reflection
• Research liberation resources
• Talk to trusted friends or counselors
• Join support communities
• Document patterns you've observed
2
📋 Planning
Preparing for Safe Transition

🎯 Goals

  • Create a comprehensive safety plan
  • Identify safe places and trusted contacts
  • Gather essential documents and resources
  • Build financial independence strategy

🔑 Key Activities

• Complete LIBERATE self-contract
• List emergency contacts & safe locations
• Secure important documents (ID, finances, etc.)
• Open separate bank account if needed
• Plan logistics (housing, transportation)
3
🚀 Action
Taking the Leap to Freedom

🎯 Goals

  • Execute your safe exit plan
  • Establish clear boundaries (no-contact or limited)
  • Secure safe and stable housing
  • Activate your support network

🔑 Key Activities

• Physical separation (if applicable)
• Communicate boundaries clearly
• Move to safe location
• Notify support network of your decision
• Block/limit contact channels as needed
4
💚 Healing
Processing & Growing

🎯 Goals

  • Process grief, trauma, and loss with support
  • Begin or continue therapy/counseling
  • Practice self-compassion and self-care
  • Rebuild self-esteem and identity

🔑 Key Activities

• Regular therapy sessions
• Join support groups (online/in-person)
• Daily journaling for processing emotions
• Self-care practices (meditation, exercise)
• Allow yourself to grieve what was lost
5
🦅 Independence
Building Your New Life

🎯 Goals

  • Achieve financial independence and stability
  • Build new social support networks
  • Rediscover personal identity and interests
  • Establish healthy routines and boundaries

🔑 Key Activities

• Secure stable employment/income
• Develop new friendships & community
• Explore hobbies and passions
• Create healthy daily routines
• Set and maintain personal boundaries
6
🌟 Flourishing
Thriving in Your Freedom

🎯 Goals

  • Create an inspiring vision for your future
  • Pursue new goals, dreams, and opportunities
  • Give back by helping others on their journey
  • Celebrate your resilience and transformation

🔑 Key Activities

• Set ambitious personal/career goals
• Mentor others in healing journeys
• Share your story (if comfortable)
• Continue personal growth practices
• Celebrate milestones and victories
