DISCERN Protocol

Diagnostic Integration for Strategic Choice in Relationship Navigation

Not all relationships should be saved. DISCERN helps you determine whether your relationship may warrant reconciliation (REPAIR) or safe separation (LIBERATE) through evidence-informed assessment across 4 diagnostic domains: Safety, Accountability, Pattern, and Impact.

🔧

REPAIR Protocol

"Restore & Rebuild Together"

A comprehensive 8-phase framework for authentic reconciliation and relationship restoration. Designed for relationships worth saving, where both parties are committed to healing.

  • ✅ Both parties want to reconcile
  • ✅ Harm was unintentional or mistake-based
  • ✅ No pattern of abuse present
  • ✅ Safety is not compromised
  • ✅ Trust can realistically be rebuilt
🚀

LIBERATE Protocol

"Break Free & Heal Independently"

An 8-phase framework for safely exiting harmful relationships and healing independently. Designed for situations where your safety, boundaries, and wellbeing must come first.

  • ⚠️ Domestic violence or abuse present
  • ⚠️ Narcissistic or toxic patterns
  • ⚠️ Repeated boundary violations
  • ⚠️ Safety is compromised
  • ⚠️ One party refuses accountability
